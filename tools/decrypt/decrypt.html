Open the document

This page opens a document sent to you by the dead man's handle. You need the file from the email (its name ends in .dmh) and the password you were given.

  1. Save the attachment from the email to your computer.
  2. Choose it below, or drag it onto the box.
  3. Type the password, then press Open. The opened document is saved to your Downloads.

Nothing leaves this computer: the page works without the internet. Please use a computer rather than a phone.
